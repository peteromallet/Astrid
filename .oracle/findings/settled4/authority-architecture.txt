1. **P0 — installed admission is not bound to the active revision at use time.**  
   §III.5 makes execution contingent on the revision being currently active/valid in `InstalledPackStore`, with matching validated provenance and permissions. §III.7’s immediate pre-use check verifies only the captured manifest and consumed resource handles; it explicitly forbids rediscovery/retry and does not revalidate active-revision state, validity, or the accepted permission receipt. Since installed revisions are selected dynamically (`astrid/core/pack/store.py:195-216`; `astrid/core/pack/discovery.py:235-257`), an active-revision switch or invalidation after snapshot capture can leave the old candidate executable for the remainder of the operation. This violates the stated single admission authority and criteria 7/12, and leaves a divergent trust decision despite the “one external admission projection” rule. The plan needs either a narrow, non-rescanning admission-state binding/check immediately before execution or evidence that `InstalledPackStore` state is immutable for the operation lifetime.

**North Star disposition:** Not CLEAN. The canonical catalog/resource architecture and typed-registry preservation align, but external execution admission is not yet durably owned by the authoritative installed-state mechanism under mutation.
