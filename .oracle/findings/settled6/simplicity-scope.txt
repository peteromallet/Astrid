**Rank 1 — P1 defect: the resource-closure audit classifies `pack.yaml` as undeclared.**

Evidence:

- §III.1 requires every retained product pack to contain `pack.yaml`.
- §IV.1 says every remaining regular non-Python file under a bundled pack root must have exactly one of three classifications: typed runtime, supplemental, or authoring-only.
- The only exclusions are `.DS_Store` and immediate `__pycache__` bytecode.
- §III.2’s `declared_paths` includes content, rendering, migration, documentation, context, supplemental, and authoring-only paths—but not the manifest itself.
- No resource type declares the manifest as an implicit resource, and no manifest exclusion is specified.

Therefore each valid pack either fails `undeclared_file`, or must self-declare its own `pack.yaml` as a supplemental resource. The latter makes the manifest a runtime resource and creates unnecessary self-referential resource/provenance semantics. This blocks criteria **1, 3, and 10**, WP2 Boundary A, and the “zero undeclared non-Python files” gate. The plan needs one explicit rule: canonical and rejected manifest basenames are audited separately and excluded from pack-resource classification, or receive a dedicated implicit-manifest classification.
